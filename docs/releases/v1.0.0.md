DATAra 1.0.0 is the build for the capstone defense on November 19, 2026. It forecasts when your measured mobile data usage will reach an amount you choose.

## What's in it

- **Accounts:** sign in with your email or username and a password, or with Google. Forgot your password? Reset it with a code sent to your email.
- **Quotas:** choose an amount in MB or GB. DATAra shows how much you have used and how much is left, counted from the moment the quota starts.
- **Forecast:** the estimated time your quota will be reached (the PET), on Home and in Quota details. It is made on your phone by an XGBoost model (`final-2026-11-17`) and updated every hour; no prediction server is involved. A personal layer learns how you use data and takes over once it is more accurate.
- **Alerts:** at 75%, 90% and 100% of your quota, and when the forecast says your quota will be reached within 24 hours or 3 hours. Each alert is sent once per quota.
- **History:** your usage over the last 24 hours and 7 days, and every past quota with how the forecast did.
- **Works offline:** tracking, forecasts and alerts keep running without internet.

## How accurate is the forecast?

Tested on 20 phones' data, with each phone left out of training: the PET was off by 10.2 hours on average, against 79.4 hours for simply repeating the last day's usage, and 86% of forecasts were within a day. Forecasts tend to come slightly early (6 hours on average), which is the safer direction for a warning.

## Privacy

Your usage data stays on your phone. DATAra's server keeps only your account (email, username and an optional display name). DATAra never reads SMS and never blocks or slows your data.

## Good to know

- **Android 8.0 or newer**, on a 64-bit ARM phone (almost every phone from the last several years).
- **Allow Usage access and notifications** when DATAra asks; without Usage access it cannot measure your data.
- **Forecasts start after about 6 measured hours.** Until then Home shows "Collecting usage".
- **On Xiaomi, POCO and Redmi phones,** turn on Autostart for DATAra (Settings → Apps → Manage apps → DATAra → Autostart) so tracking restarts after the phone closes it.
- **One phone per account for now:** your data isn't synced, so signing in on a second phone starts empty.
- **Installing over an older test build:** if Android says the app conflicts with an existing package, the old build was signed with a different key. Uninstalling it removes its data.
